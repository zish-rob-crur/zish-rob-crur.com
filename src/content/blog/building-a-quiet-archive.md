---
title: 'Building a quiet archive'
description: 'Why this site exists, what belongs here, and what it is trying to avoid.'
pubDate: '2026-03-20'
---

This site starts from a simple preference: fewer things, but sharper ones.

I like writing that can breathe. I like software that does not need to announce itself every few
seconds. I like defaults that get quieter over time instead of louder.

So this archive is for notes that stay useful after the first week. Some posts will be technical.
Some will be about tools, habits, and setup. Some will just be attempts to make an idea precise
enough to keep.

I am not trying to publish at maximum velocity.

I would rather publish something measured than something inflated. I would rather keep a small
body of writing that still feels true a year later.

That is the point of this place.
